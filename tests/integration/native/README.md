# Native acceptance assembly

`python3 tests/integration/native/runner.py --check-plan` validates requirement accounting without network, process, database or browser activity. A valid plan is preparation only. All 291 bindings currently remain deferred: the named final inventory and exact provider entry points were not supplied in the input tree. Each of the 64 journey slots now names feature content, a rulebook section and distinct positive, negative and recovery outcomes. This is a provisional mapping from the supplied rulebook; final inventory alignment and exact provider contracts remain deferred. Numbered identifiers remain stable.

`python3 tests/integration/native/runner.py --origin http://localhost:3000 --run-dir "$RUN" --require-all` consumes existing evidence only. It never starts services, executes scenarios, changes provider files or grants execution clearance. Missing evidence or any unbound requirement returns 1. Even syntactically complete evidence returns 1 with provider_outcome_contracts_deferred until exact provider outcome decoders are integrated; labels and hashes alone never establish acceptance. There is no partial acceptance mode. The diagnostic execution stage remains skipped until prerequisites land and execution is separately authorized.

## Published contract

`required-proofs.json` uses `native-plan.v1`. Each requirement names its owning provider, scenario, versioned evidence contract, positive/negative/recovery assertions and required native lifecycle dependencies N04 through N09. A future reviewed binding supplies a repository-relative provider entrypoint and exact 40-character source revision together. Null values mean missing capability, never optional coverage. Provider content stays with E06, A10, E04, E08 and E09; this manifest neither copies nor replaces it.

The run directory supplies `provider-evidence.json` with `schema_version: native-run.v1`, a nonempty unique `run_id` and `records`. Each record supplies `schema_version: native-provider.v1`, `requirement`, `provider`, `scenario`, `source_revision`, `entrypoint`, the exact invocation `origin` and `run_id`, `execution: real_provider`, `result: passed`, and `fixture_only: false`. `assertions` maps every required assertion to literal true; `lifecycle` maps every required dependency to passed. `artifacts` is a nonempty list of relative `path` and exact `sha256` pairs within the run directory. Unknown or duplicate requirements reject the run. Artifacts are read only and never copied into the repository.

Physical evidence additionally names `device_id`, `operator_reference` and `captured_at`. Load/speed evidence names `dataset_sha256`, `connection` and `sample_count`; speed evidence records integer `maximum_ms` within the stated 2000/30000 limits. Dataset load limits still require the owning provider's published dataset and target contract. Phone journeys require both locales/themes, keyboard, reduced motion, 390-pixel geometry, in-place input preservation and private-memory assertions. Physical-device evidence remains separately attributable.

These fields are a proposed consumer contract, not an assertion that provider interfaces have landed. Artifact hashes bind bytes, not truth: independent review of real provider assertions is required before binding. In particular, provider checks must demonstrate actual retained economics, balanced Orders journal entries, principal-only refunds, confirmed failed-inspection collection, current custody, receipt, independent review, quality/rework and conditional payout. An awaiting-payment reservation is not a completed purchase. A health response, prepared successful state or local fixture is insufficient.

## Outcome observations

Every hashed artifact must contain a `native-outcome.v1` JSON observation, bound to `requirement`, `source_revision`, `run_id` and `origin`. Empty JSON is rejected. Records repeat the manifest's `content` exactly. `cases` contains exactly `positive`, `negative` and `recovery`; each names its content assertion and a distinct stable `operation_id`. Recovery repeats that reference in `retry_operation_id`. Negative observations use the manifest’s exact `negative_state` and `negative_effects`. Most require denial without effects; a mismatched callback requires a recorded reconciliation hold, held settlement and `mutated: true`. Any additional effect is rejected.

Cases carry `watch_id`, `state`, `quality`, `window_expired`, `hold`, `authority`, `money` and `custody`. Authority binds current permission and equal expected/current revisions, with distinct reviewer identities excluding the acting customer. Money uses nonnegative integer USD cents for principal, collected fees, confirmed/reserved refunds, refunded fees and payout; the single simulated journal has signed integer entries summing to zero. Its `operation` is `none`, `collection`, `refund` or `payout`. No-posting operations use an empty journal; collection postings total principal plus collected fees, refund postings total confirmed refunded principal, and payout postings total payout. Unpaid reservations permit retained principal but no collection, refund or payout. Refund reservations plus confirmed refunds cannot exceed principal, and refunded fees must be zero. Custody requires an actual receipt for the same watch and authorized recipient; dispatch scans do not establish receipt. Return dispatch requires confirmed collection and a return instruction. Positive and recovery states must match the manifest, with sale allocations remaining awaiting payment. The separate `settlement_state` cannot claim completion from that reservation. Completion requires paid held funds, actual receipt, passed quality, expired window and no hold. A payout observation requires paid-out state.

These proposed observations are bounded consumer fixtures, not independently verified provider facts or a replacement for provider-specific decoders. Connected acceptance remains nonzero even when these structural and invariant checks pass. Review must bind actual operations, source revisions and provider-specific retained economics before removing the deferred gate. No prerequisite is claimed to have landed.

Settlement states are limited to awaiting payment, held funds, completion, payout, held review and refund. Positive journal postings must match the observed operation amount. Failed-inspection collection is checked even before dispatch; receipt and payout requirements retain their specific obligations. These constraints deliberately support a bounded proposed observation shape; final provider journal decoding remains deferred.

Recovery includes distinct `original` and `retry` observations with increasing integer `sequence`, unique nonempty `observation_id`, identical `operation_id`, and the same requirement/source/run/origin binding. Each embeds the exact retained case outcome (excluding original/retry observations and retry_operation_id). Original `new_effect_ids` are unique; retry `new_effect_ids` must be empty. Changed references, retained outcomes or duplicate effects reject the evidence. These are proposed attributable observations; provider decoding and independent verification remain deferred.

Denied and held negative cases include attributable `before` and `after` observations bound to the requirement, source revision, run, origin and unchanged operation reference. Distinct observation identifiers and increasing sequences are required. Financial and custody snapshots must remain identical, financial operation must be `none`, and journal postings must be empty. Only the exact declared reconciliation hold effect is permitted. Existing balances are observations, never fresh collection or payout evidence.

Refund positive/recovery cases require a nonzero confirmed principal refund, matching refund postings, refunded settlement and an explicit decision reference authorizing that amount against the same operation. Ordinary collection and a zero refund cannot satisfy this requirement.

The `original_crosswalk` retains all 20 original required capabilities by source digest and identifies related journey slots. It retains the original validation and case entry points read-only; these links are scope references, not equivalent replacement scenarios. Missing or stale accounting rejects the plan. The original 64-journey final inventory and exact provider tests are unavailable in this input, so final alignment remains explicitly deferred through `original_inventory_alignment_deferred`. No provisional slot removes an original negative or recovery obligation; exact case-level binding remains required before connected acceptance.

Payment confirmation observations require retained order, quote/revision, buyer, watch, route and USD component amounts. The confirmation repeats those bindings and the stable case operation, confirms held funds at a positive revision and supplies collection postings for the exact customer total. Zero principal and empty confirmation postings are rejected. Watch buyer fees are 3%; watch seller and job business fees are 5%, rounded half up to cents. Jobs have no buyer surcharge. `business_fee_receivable_minor` records the separate business obligation, never collected customer cash; principal refunds do not deduct it.

All authority observations require current permission and matching revisions. Only independent brand approval, brand approval, paid customization, paid customization jobs and purchase-then-customization require four explicit `roles`: assessor, brand_approver, quality_reviewer and eligibility_reviewer. Each binds its role, actor, organization, watch subject, active status, membership_revision/current_membership_revision and assignment_revision/current_assignment_revision. All revisions must be current positive integers; all four people must differ from each other and the customer. These proposed bindings remain subject to real-provider decoding and independent review.

Economics requirements follow capability: allocation, reservation, payment confirmation, journal, failed inspection, refund, payout, paid customization and each paid service type require retained positive principal and exact fees. Any observation reporting positive principal also receives the same economics checks. Other capabilities may report zero principal, zero financial components, unpaid state and no postings or retained terms; `not_applicable` settlement records no commercial completion. Registration and search therefore require neither invented purchases nor job reviewers. Ordinary purchases retain current authority checks without job-review roles. Payment confirmation also compares `watch_id` directly with the retained terms.

Paid customization and every paid service type require positive and recovery observations with held payment, collection or payout postings, completed or paid-out settlement, passed quality, actual receipt, expired dispute window and no hold. Service work requires current scoped assessor, brand approver and quality reviewer bindings with distinct people independent of the customer; any supplied eligibility reviewer must also be current and independent. Customization retains its separate required eligibility reviewer. Negative cases remain denied or held observations, never completed work.

Paid service and customization observations bind retained economics to `route: job` in positive, negative and recovery cases. The customer total equals principal with no buyer surcharge; substituting watch economics is rejected even when its amounts and journal balance internally.

Every commercial observation binds retained buyer and watch references to the observed buyer and watch, including negative and recovery cases. Mismatched references reject the observation before payment-confirmation-specific validation.
