# Bid notices v2

Inbox consumes auction.outbid.v2 only from the authenticated auction producer.
The auction producer emits separate occurrences for displaced holders and
immediately beaten challengers. Each occurrence names its own recipient,
auction, sequence, revision and reason. The schema rejects additional fields.
No private ceiling is accepted, persisted or forwarded. Opening the notice
requires current access and current auction state. Delivery preferences do not
suppress these required notices. Repeated occurrences keep the first notice.

The `bid-notices-v2` destination posts `auction.bid-outbid.v2` to
`/internal/v1/events` with service subject `auction` and audience
`communications`. Its exact payload names `auction_id`, `listing_id`,
`bidder_id`, `sequence`, `revision`, `current_minor` and `currency`. The
auction ID is the envelope subject. This immediately beaten bidder event
reconciles the accepted bid's outbid occurrence by auction, sequence and bidder.
It can retry until that occurrence exists. The accepted event's durable bridge
remains responsible for the single Inbox notice.
