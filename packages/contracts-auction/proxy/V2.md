# Private maximum v2

The canonical transaction selects `auction.proxy.v2` for ordinary bids and
private maxima. `proxy.py:calculate_v2` is pure; `auction.proxy.validate.v2`
and the transaction validator verify the result before persistence. Only
verified buyer ceilings enter the hook. Earlier compatibility rows enter it
only when the stored accepted command can be distinguished from an ordinary
bid using the original receipt and public bid sequence.

The input is the private state plus the next canonical receipt sequence.
Only the leader may have an active ceiling. Inactive ceilings remain stored
at their accepted amount and sequence until explicitly raised. A manual bid
cannot add, raise or reactivate one, even if it regains the lead. The leader
may have no ceiling. Only the owner can raise a ceiling and the raise must
cover the public price. A leader's private raise produces no public bid.

Opening bids may equal the opening price. After that the current price plus
its increment is the next minimum. A competing maximum challenges at the
higher of that minimum and the lower of the incoming and incumbent active
ceilings; without an active incumbent ceiling it challenges at the minimum.
Select the response increment at the challenge amount. If the incumbent
cannot respond with a full step at or below its ceiling, the challenge wins,
including a later equal maximum that satisfies the current next minimum.
Historical public rows can contain equal prices with distinct sequences; keep
them in history. An accepted sequence can advance without adding a public row.

Public bid rows contain only bidder ID, amount in cents and automatic status.
Public history masks bidder IDs. Private maxima, flags, quote references and
request fingerprints do not enter public history, events or logs. The
canonical accepted event binds the submitting, previous leading and final
leading bidders. The existing Communications consumer derives both displaced
and immediately beaten outbid notices from these identities and deduplicates
by event, recipient and kind. `fixtures/notices.v2.json` records these cases.

## Current private transport

These routes are scoped to the current individual buyer and the selected
auction, including after close. Current identity, organization, ownership and
record authority are rechecked; responses are private and not cacheable.

- `GET /v2/auctions/{id}/maximum`: exact own ceiling, active flag, current
  standing (`leading`, `outbid` or `null`), auction revision and physical
  watch ID. An inactive ceiling does not imply the buyer is outbid.
- `POST /v2/auctions/{id}/maximum`: schema in `command.v2.schema.json`. The
  UUID `Idempotency-Key` is the operation ID. The command binds the auction's
  physical watch, expected revision, reviewed Pricing quote and its quote and
  policy revisions. A caller may send only amount, quote ID and expected auction
  revision; Auction derives the remaining bindings from its record and the
  reviewed Pricing quote. If supplied, all three optional bindings must match.
  Pricing uses `usd-v2` for the USD bid preview; the policy
  revision is the string representation of its numeric policy revision, not
  the fee schedule ID. The provider rechecks Pricing and Auction authority.
- `GET /v2/auctions/{id}/maximum/operations/{operation_id}`: the exact bound
  cap-free receipt specified in `operation.v2.schema.json`. An accepted
  receipt advances the auction revision and survives restart. A missing
  provider or unverified result is unavailable, not an acceptance.

A missing lookup is not a decision. After a lost response, replay the exact
original POST on that auction with the same body and key, including after
close or relisting. Authenticate and check record access before reading the
receipt. Look up a matching durable receipt under the canonical operation lock
before checking fresh admission. If no receipt exists, decide against current
revision, auction mode, server time, quote and permissions. Store a bound
terminal denial or stale receipt when definitive, under the same key, so a
delayed original delivery cannot be admitted. Conflicting reuse denies.
Uncertain results remain pending. The browser does not treat an HTTP status
alone as a terminal result.

The existing buyer-scoped `POST /v1/auctions/{id}/maximum` also carries these
operations as explicit `read_v2`, `submit_v2` and `lookup_v2` envelopes. Submit
uses `command`; lookup uses `operation_id`. `read_v2` without current admission
can return only schema version 1, auction ID, actor ID and record revision to
confirm that historical recovery is allowed; it does not authorize a bid or
expose a ceiling. A registered provider's failure closes private access.

## Earlier receipts

The registered route can recover earlier decisions without fresh old-rule
admission. `lookup_v1` takes the original `operation_key` and `command` and
verifies the stored fingerprint for the maximum kind and auction. A missing
or mismatched receipt looks absent. `recover_v1` takes the same command with
its original `Idempotency-Key`. Under the existing operation lock it returns
an existing terminal receipt or atomically records `legacy_maximum_retired` if
absent. A pending receipt is not an error or a terminal result. Lookup is
read-only; a retry of a matching pending command may call the current
provider's `reconcile_legacy_current(request, auction_id, command)`. That
operation rechecks current access and reconciles only the persisted intent
with the same key and fingerprint, even after the window or quote expires.
It may not start fresh legacy admission. The route reads the bound durable
receipt again after reconciliation; if the provider is not yet available,
the pending receipt remains pending. A later identical request cannot change
a terminal decision. `list_bid_receipts_v1`
returns at most 20 recorded own bid decisions, most recent first. Pass the
returned `next_cursor` as `before`. The list omits keys, amounts, private
quote references and hashes; it never replays a bid.

Unwrapped requests first look for the matching authorized receipt. A fresh
unwrapped request without the canonical provider stays unavailable. To
support it after cutover, the provider must implement `submit_legacy_current`
with the original key and fingerprint, resolving the watch and reviewed quote
from their sources. The route then reads the resulting durable receipt; a
provider return value does not establish that it committed. The transport has
one 600 ms budget across access, replay, admission and receipt verification.

## Canonical activation

Register `proxy_private_v2` with `read_current`, `submit_current` and
`lookup_current`. Advertise `bid_admission_version = "auction.proxy.v2"` only
when both bid kinds use the D27 transition and validator, and the state update,
public bids, receipt and outbox are atomic under the existing canonical lock.
Resolve the provenance of old compatibility rows before treating one as a
private ceiling. Keep ambiguous rows out of competition. Preserve the accepted
public history, original receipt keys and current recovery scope. Do not alter
the Commerce commitment or auction end. No fresh v2 submission is allowed
without both the canonical version and a current provider.

Run the transaction and notice boundary cases in `tests/lanes/L16/A07/test_adapters.py`
against the activated runtime. Required cases
include: Maya's 450000 ceiling responding to 420000 by a full step, the 445000
and 450000 challenges displacing that ceiling, notice to the beaten challenger,
opening bid at the opening price, deliberate replay, and no second receipt,
bid or notice on duplicate delivery. In addition, verify own read-back, closing,
relisting, restart, denied access, receipt recovery and fee expiry through the
registered buyer and ordinary bid routes. Pure fixtures are not evidence of
connected activation.

The buyer view retains only stable operation bindings (not amounts, tokens or
private evidence) across reload. It uses the original owner-scoped reviewed
quote to reconstruct the amount for replay. Each operation and stage has its
own storage key. Display and replay require a fresh check of access to the
original auction. Without it, or without the exact quote, recovery stays
unavailable or pending. A bound terminal result clears that request.
