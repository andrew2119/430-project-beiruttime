# Offers v2 — retained USD terms

Source baseline 02387b0; revised implementation in progress. Active public routes
remain `/v1/offers`; command schema version 2 removes client `expires_at`.
MakeOffer accepts listing_id, listing_revision, amount_minor (integer USD cents),
currency USD, schedule_id usd-v2, fee_quote_id. Send requires a reviewed, unexpired
15-minute exact offer preview. The server sets the sent deadline to its own send
time plus 48 hours; the preview's deadline no longer limits seller acceptance.

Catalog's canonical modes are fixed_price and offer; Orders' matching physical
watch modes are fixed and seller_base. Auctions are never offerable. Orders alone
serializes exclusive allocation; Pricing records acceptance_pending before its
bounded call and retries the same immutable operation on uncertainty. Admission
strictly before the offer deadline retains accepted economics without expiry.
Orders must consume pricing-current/v3 and start a usable payment clock only when
the required payment-action notice is durably available to that buyer.

Any confirmed competing reservation or withdrawal closes open offers and emits
one required closure fact per affected buyer in the same transaction. A bounded
worker reads Orders' exact watch/listing/revision status and Catalog's publication
status, including Buy Now/withdrawal routes, and retries unknown results. Reserved
or withdrawn are facts; a timeout, hidden record, missing response or generic fence
is not a confirmed withdrawal. No invented order/payment is shown. Consumers must
expose confirmed withdrawal status through one of those authoritative projections.

`POST /v1/offers/{id}/unblock` requires current buyer/seller access and a stable
Idempotency-Key. It deletes only the caller's blocker_scope. The other party's
block remains effective. Both sides may block independently. Active custody and
support obligations are outside this ordinary-offer block.

Sent economics, parties and expiration are immutable. Legacy sent deadlines and
snapshots remain unchanged, never silently extended or converted. Non-USD records
are outside active forms/financial receipts and require recorded reconciliation.
Existing v1 notice payloads remain unchanged: opaque references, revisions and
state only, no exact prices or private notes. Pricing never collects funds.
