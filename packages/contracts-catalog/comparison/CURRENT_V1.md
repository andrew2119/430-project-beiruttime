# Public comparison v1

`POST /v1/comparison/operations/{operation_id}` is a read operation and its current-operation lookup. The immutable body is `CompareRequest` in schema.py. Reuse the same reference and body after a lost response, timeout or restart. It creates no saved selection, commitment, receipt, quote or financial authority. Duplicate watch identifiers and more than three selections are rejected. Empty selections return an empty comparison. No request field grants identity or business access.

Every lookup rechecks each current public record and source revision. It never replays retained facts as current availability. Expected revisions produce a fact-free stale column when changed; explicit review omits that expectation on the next read. Hidden, sold, reserved, removed and missing records produce the same fact-free unavailable column. A denied dependency produces denied; an uncertain dependency produces pending. No private source body, identity, evidence reference, full serial or former-owner detail may enter output, errors or logs. There is no purchase action in this interface.

The provider boundary is `PublicSnapshot` in the comparison module. It must be composed from current publication, physical-watch commitment, business status, public specifications and exact versioned economics. It may supply only approved public bilingual facts. The provider must bind listing, physical watch, publication, availability and selected business consistently, including current revisions. An expired snapshot at equality is unavailable. All clock values are authoritative UTC. Provider absence is pending, never synthetic success. A 750ms budget bounds each sequential provider read; at most three reads and zero automatic retries occur.

Economics are USD integer cents from a retained source version, presented as an indicative comparison. Missing economics remain unavailable. They are never recalculated into an executable price here. Seller approval and business assurance never imply independent watch authentication. A declaration of no modification is not an originality guarantee. Missing papers explicitly mean no documents provided.

Fixtures are synthetic, deterministic public examples. They prove contract behavior only. Real publication, commitment, pricing and business adapters, runtime registration and connected permission/restart journeys remain required before connected acceptance.
