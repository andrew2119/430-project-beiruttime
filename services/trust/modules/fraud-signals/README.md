# Fraud signal composition

The host calls create_router with the v1 Scan, Photo and Bid contract types and provides a Store on a private durable service volume. No runtime registration is changed by this module.

The host's fraud_signal_provider.current(request, body) is an authenticated asynchronous context manager. It must obtain current identity, selected organization, independent assignment and record permissions, resolve operation lookup to its retained scope without exposing its existence, and hold that authority/source lease until the local transaction completes. Context includes actor_id, organization_id, subject_id, watch_id, seller_id, source_revision, record_revision, assigned, permitted, parties and an aware valid_until. Parties must be an explicit nonempty list of nonempty identifiers including seller, subject and all bidders, even when no bids exist. Missing or malformed independence facts deny scans, replay, lookup and pagination. Scan bidders absent from that list deny the scan. Source facts must be complete within the declared bounds and limited to the current seller and watch investigation. A lease cannot come from client claims, cached membership or a browser projection.

The local receipt is the only side effect. Review is due within 24 hours; the assigned reviewer is the responsible next actor. Escalation after 48 hours without progress belongs to the operations steward. No automatic restriction, financial action or private evidence publication is emitted. Source acquisition, service authentication, durable-volume deployment, retention scheduling and the shared escalation connection require connected verification before acceptance.
